Your scroll is the play button.

Meet BR-Burger—an AI-assisted concept website for a fictional premium fast-food brand.

The idea: make exploring a menu feel like watching a product launch.

🍔 Scroll-controlled burger assembly
🎬 Seven cinematic chapters
🧀 A rotating 3D menu with ten items
✨ Animated typography and responsive interactions

The biggest technical challenge was keeping scroll position, video seeking, and 3D motion in sync. Loading animation work only as each chapter approaches made a real difference to startup performance.

Built with Next.js, GSAP, Lenis, Framer Motion, and Three.js.

Which part would you refine next: the hero, the transitions, or the 3D menu?
