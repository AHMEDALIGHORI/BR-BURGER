# LinkedIn publishing pack

- Upload `BR-Burger-LinkedIn-30s.mp4` as a native video post.
- Select `BR-Burger-LinkedIn-cover.jpg` as the optional cover.
- Copy the text from `LinkedIn-caption.txt`; replace any phrasing that does not sound like your own voice.
- The video is 30 seconds, portrait 1080 × 1920, H.264/AAC, 30 fps. The on-screen titles communicate the story without requiring sound. The audio is an original procedural composition, not a third-party music track.
- The website is a fictional, AI-assisted concept. The copy identifies it accordingly.
- If you have a public live demo, add its actual URL. `localhost:3000` works only on your own computer and is not a public demo link.
- Respond personally to relevant questions and technical feedback. Avoid mass-tagging unrelated people, automated comments, or engagement pods.

No caption, hashtag set, or posting time can guarantee one million impressions. These hashtags describe the project; they are not a reach guarantee.
